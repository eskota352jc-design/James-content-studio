Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

James Content Studio is a creator content management service designed to help authorized users review account information, understand content performance, prepare original media, and publish content when they explicitly choose to do so.

When a user connects a supported social account, the service may process profile information, authorized account statistics, information about posts, media selected by the user, captions and publishing settings. Access is limited to permissions the user authorizes through the platform's authentication process.

We use this information only to provide requested content-management features, including displaying account information, analyzing authorized performance data, preparing posts and carrying out user-initiated publishing actions. We do not ask users to provide their social-platform passwords.

Users control what content they choose to prepare and publish. We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate authentication, hosting, storage and authorized platform integrations.

Users may revoke platform authorization through the applicable platform account settings. Requests concerning access, correction or deletion of data associated with this service may be submitted through the service operator's support channel when available. Data is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security.

This policy may be updated as the service develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
