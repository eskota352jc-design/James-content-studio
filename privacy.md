# James Content Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

James Content Studio is a personal content management tool designed to help manage, analyze, prepare, and publish social media content.

## Information Accessed

When a user connects a social media account, James Content Studio may access information authorized by the user through the platform's official authentication system.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include:

- Basic profile information
- Profile and account statistics
- Information about published content
- Content performance information
- Information necessary to prepare or publish content

James Content Studio does not require users to provide their social media account passwords directly.

## How Information Is Used

Authorized information is used only to provide content management functionality, including analyzing content, assisting with content creation, retrieving account and content information, and publishing content when requested by the user.

## Third-Party Services

James Content Studio may use third-party services and APIs to provide its functionality. These services may process information as necessary to perform authorized actions.

Use of TikTok information is also subject to TikTok's applicable developer terms and policies.

## Data Sharing

James Content Studio does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to authorized service providers when necessary to provide requested functionality.

## Account Authorization

Access to connected social media accounts occurs through the applicable platform's authorization process.

Users may revoke access through the applicable social media platform or connected service.

## Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary to provide the service or fulfill authorized requests, subject to the operation and retention practices of connected third-party services.

## Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through James Content Studio. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as James Content Studio develops or its functionality changes.

## Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the operator of James Content Studio.
